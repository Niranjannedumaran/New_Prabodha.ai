### 2. The Reimagined Homepage Hero Composition

#### Desktop Experience (1440px Viewport Benchmark)
- **Visual Stance:** Expansive, widescreen architectural canvas set against Deep Obsidian (`#0B0A0F`), layered with faint structural hairlines (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`).
- **Typography:**
  - Massive, brutalist display headline set in high-contrast pure white:
    ```
    LEARN.
    PREPARE.
    GROW. EVOLVE.
    ```
  - Words rise from `overflow-hidden` mask containers using GSAP with custom exponential easing (`[0.16, 1, 0.3, 1]`).
  - The final word *“Evolve.”* is accented with the signature Newsreader editorial italic serif in kinetic violet (`#B99CFF`).
- **Mascot & Graphical Integration:**
  - The official student mascot is no longer trapped inside a rounded card. He stands proudly grounded on an architectural base pedestal on the right, integrated directly into the canvas.
  - The 3D Gyroscopic Intelligence Universe SVG orbits gracefully behind him, representing the multi-axis knowledge network.
  - An organic feather gradient (`mask-image: linear-gradient(to bottom, black 85%, transparent 100%)`) eliminates any harsh cropped edges.
- **Action Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Magnetic Pill: Obsidian/White with specular sheen: `[ Explore the Platform ➔ ]`.
  - Secondary Invariant Anchor: Subtle frosted glass: `[ Inspect Socratic Demo ✧ ]`.
  - Dual-Audience Telemetry Badge: `15 Years Past Papers Vectorized • 98.1% Invariant Convergence`.

#### Mobile Experience (390px Viewport Benchmark)
- **Visual Stance:** Pure vertical rhythm with zero horizontal overflow (`max-w-full`, `overflow-x: hidden`).
- **Typography:** Display scale recalibrates to a tight, high-impact `38px–44px` size with tight line-height (`1.05`), ensuring the four key words command the first screen without pushing the primary CTA below the fold.
- **Mascot Presentation:** The student mascot is positioned directly below the headline and CTA buttons, framed within a grounded vertical base dock. Pointer-based mouse parallax is disabled for 60 FPS touch scrolling.
- **Touch Targets:** All interactive triggers maintain minimum `48px` height for seamless thumb operation.

### 3. Pacing & Sectional Rhythm Across the Page

To ensure the website feels like one continuous, purposeful narrative rather than a collection of disjointed sections, the page alternates intentionally between high-contrast surfaces:

1. **Section 01 — Global Navigation:** Ultra-clean floating glass bar with high-contrast logo, theme toggle, and audio switch.
2. **Section 02 — Hero (Cinematic Obsidian):** Kinetic typography, student mascot composition, and Gyroscopic Intelligence Universe.
3. **Curriculum Ticker:** Sideways-sliding continuous ticker spanning UPSC CSE, RBI Grade B, GATE CS, and CAT Analytics.
4. **Section 03 — The Structural Problem (Editorial Bone Canvas):** Stark, asymmetric layout breaking down the 4 failure modes of conventional coaching (information overload, rote tricks, career disconnect, burnout).
5. **Section 04 — Prabodha's Approach (Kinetic Clarity Ribbon):** Continuous vector pipeline weaving Curiosity → Understanding → Practice → Preparation → Growth.
6. **Section 05 — The Four Connected Pillars (Clean UI Discipline):** Exam Intelligence, AI Learning, Career Intelligence, and AI Mentorship with live SVG vector architecture switcher.
7. **Section 06 — Socratic Reasoning Sandbox (Interactive Lab):** Live demonstration of how misconceptions resolve into formal invariants.
8. **Section 07 — The Learner Journey (First-Principles Simulation):** 5-stage progression with pedagogical proofs.
9. **Section 08 — Company Vision & Institutional Direction (Telemetry Grid):** Defensible technological moats and 15-year syllabus archives for strategic partners and investors.
10. **Section 09 — Examination Topology Matrix:** Deep-dive dossier breakdown across statutory syllabuses.
11. **Section 10 — Frequently Asked Questions:** Accessible, keyboard-navigable search accordion.
12. **Section 11 — Closing Invitation (Cinematic Obsidian Dock):** "Grow with direction. Master the invariants." with grounded triumphant mascot.
13. **Section 12 — Platform Footer:** Structured directory, contact channels, and legal policies.
